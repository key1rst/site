Product Requirements Document (PRD)
International Industrial Oils Trading Platform & CRM

Version: 1.0
Product Type: B2B Trading Platform + Corporate Website + CRM
Target Market: International
Business Model: Sell company-owned products to customers + purchase products from suppliers
Platform Type: Single-Company Trading Platform
Primary Language: English
Future Languages: Persian, Arabic, Turkish, Chinese, Russian, French

1. Executive Summary

The objective of this project is to design and develop an integrated international B2B platform for trading products related to:

- Base Oils
- Industrial Lubricants
- Greases
- Process Oils
- RPO
- White Oil
- Antifreeze
- Petroleum and petrochemical-related products

This platform is not a multi-vendor marketplace.

The company that owns the platform will be the primary commercial party in each transaction.

The platform must support two major commercial flows:

Buy From Us

Customers can review the companyâ€™s available or supplyable products and submit purchase requests or RFQs.

Sell To Us

Suppliers can submit product offers to sell materials directly to the company.

All purchase requests and supplier offers must automatically enter the CRM so that the Sales and Procurement teams can manage the full transaction lifecycle from initial inquiry to negotiation, contract, shipment, and completion.

2. Product Vision

Build a professional B2B platform that becomes the digital commercial operating system of the company.

The final product should allow the business to:

- Present its products professionally
- Display available inventory
- Receive purchase requests
- Receive supplier offers
- Manage customers
- Manage suppliers
- Create quotations
- Track negotiations
- Manage documents
- Manage inventory
- Create tasks and follow-ups
- Track sales and procurement performance
- Generate management reports

The website and CRM should not function as separate systems.

They should operate as one integrated business ecosystem.

3. Business Objectives

The primary business objectives are:

1. Increase the number of qualified international leads.
2. Convert website visitors into purchase inquiries.
3. Create a structured channel for receiving supplier offers.
4. Reduce dependency on WhatsApp, email, spreadsheets, and manual tracking.
5. Build a centralized database of customers and suppliers.
6. Manage the complete sales pipeline.
7. Manage the complete procurement pipeline.
8. Reduce RFQ response time.
9. Improve quotation-to-deal conversion.
10. Build a professional and trustworthy international company image.
11. Create a scalable technology foundation for future integrations with ERP, accounting, logistics, BI, and other systems.

4. Product Principles

The product must be designed and developed around the following principles.

Trust First
Trust is one of the most important factors in international petroleum and industrial oil trading.

B2B First
The platform must not look or behave like a typical consumer e-commerce store.

Fast RFQ
Customers should be able to submit purchase requests with minimal friction.

Technical Transparency
Product specifications and technical information must be clear, structured, and professional.

CRM-Centric
Every meaningful commercial interaction should be captured inside the CRM.

Mobile Friendly
A significant number of B2B users may access the platform from mobile devices and communicate through WhatsApp.

Scalable Architecture
The platform must support future expansion into additional products, languages, countries, employees, offices, workflows, and modules.

123. Key Product Recommendation

The first release should not become unnecessarily complex.

The MVP should focus on three major outcomes:

1. Professional presentation of products and inventory
2. Receiving Buy Requests and Sell Offers
3. Managing those requests effectively inside the CRM

Once these three areas are working reliably, the company can expand into Quotations, Customer Portal, Supplier Portal, Compliance, Automation, and third-party integrations.

124. North Star Metric

The primary product success metric should be:

The number of qualified commercial inquiries that enter the CRM through the platform and successfully convert into real transactions.

The website design, user experience, content strategy, CRM, sales workflow, and procurement workflow should all be optimized around this metric.
